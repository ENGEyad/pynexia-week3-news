Native Android News App built with Java, XML and Retrofit. Loads recent technology, business or sports headlines from the GDELT DOC JSON API.

Swipe to refresh. RecyclerView displays headline, publisher domain, date and image loaded with Glide. Select an item for story details and open the original publisher page.

Run the news module in Android Studio with JDK 17/21 and Android SDK 35 on Android 8.0/API 26 or later. Internet access is needed.

Build: assembleDebug passed. Automated tests were not run. Lint findings are included in the repository and report.

Availability, article coverage and images depend on GDELT and external publishers. Full article text remains on the publisher website.

Hosted page includes the APK, source archive, report and screenshots.
